Core Capabilities

Built for Real Threat Scenarios

AEGIS XII combines packet inspection, process awareness, and an intelligent rule engine into a single lightweight executable.

Deep Packet Inspection

Every packet is analyzed at the application layer. Malicious payloads are identified and dropped before they reach your system.

Real-Time Process Monitor

Maps every active process to its network connections. Instantly block or allow entire applications with a single click in the dashboard.

Adaptive Rule Engine

Define IP, port, protocol, and process-level rules. Rules are loaded from a plain-text config and hot-applied without restarting.

Geo-IP Blocking

Block entire countries or CIDR ranges in one step. Ideal for stopping nation-state lateral movement and botnets at the edge.

Threat Auto-Ban

IPs triggering port-scan or flood heuristics are automatically banned. Bans are timed and expire cleanly — zero manual intervention.

Silent Background Operation

No console window. No system tray clutter. AEGIS XII runs silently as a background process and opens its live dashboard directly in your browser.

Under The Hood

How AEGIS XII Works

01

Capture

AEGIS XII binds to your network adapter using raw sockets (Windows) or NFQUEUE (Linux) and intercepts every packet before it is processed.

→
02

Inspect

The DPI engine and rule engine evaluate each packet against your configured rules, geo-blocks, rate-limits, and behavioral heuristics.

→
03

Verdict

Packets are either allowed or dropped. All events are logged, stats are updated live, and the dashboard reflects the decision instantly.
