Comprehensive Defense

Every module in AEGIS XII is designed to intercept threats before they reach the OS layer, utilizing a seamless desktop architecture.

Deep Packet Inspection (DPI)

Evaluate traffic payloads in real-time. The DPI engine inspects application-layer data to identify and drop zero-day exploits and malware signatures instantly.

DNS Firewall

Intercepts and blocks outbound requests to malicious or unverified domains.

MAC Watchdog

Monitors layer-2 traffic to detect and halt ARP spoofing and MITM attacks.

Autonomous Threat Banning

Built-in heuristics detect port scans and volumetric floods. Malicious actors are automatically isolated and banned at the network edge without manual intervention.

Raw Socket Interception

AEGIS XII bypasses standard user-space APIs. By hooking directly into the network adapter, it acts as the final gatekeeper for inbound and outbound traffic.
